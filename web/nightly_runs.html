How to read this page
  • The iMac runs the pipeline every night at 9 pm. It looks for new photos and videos on the NAS, analyses them and makes them searchable on the Content Desk.
  • Finished properly: every step ended without errors. Had problems: a batch stopped, a drive disconnected, or the run was stopped on purpose; the next run picks up where it left off.
  • No run: nothing was recorded for that night. Usually the iMac was off or asleep. A run that could not start because the NAS or work drive was missing shows the reason in the table.
  • New files are files found on the NAS that night. Added and Searchable count the files of that night's batches that reached the library and the search page.
  • Runs before 18 Nov 2026 were copied from the iMac's own log file; two runs that were cut off were added from their logs.
